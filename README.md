Stride
A quiet step tracker for the walking you already do. Open it, move, and let the day add up — no feeds, no noise.

Version 1.0

What it does
Steps from your phone

Your phone's motion sensor does the counting. No wearable, no account with a watch app.

A goal you can live with

Set a daily target, watch the ring fill, and keep a streak going one ordinary day at a time.

More than a number

Distance, active minutes and calories are estimated from your steps and stride length.

Private by design

No location tracking. Your days are stored on your own account and only you can open them.

How counting works

Your phone's accelerometer reports small movements many times a second.

Stride watches for the up-and-down rhythm of a footfall and ignores shake and fidget.

Each detected step is added to today's count, then saved to your history within seconds.
Estimates are close, not exact — a dedicated pedometer will always be a little stricter.
